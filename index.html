Hi, I'm Sarah.

I learned to bake in my grandmother's farmhouse kitchen outside Red Deer, spent six years in Vancouver bakeries, and came home to the Bow Valley to open the shop I always wanted to walk into.

Maple & Main is small on purpose: one oven, one starter named Maple, and a team of four. If something's not right with your order, tell us at the counter — or send us a message. We read everything, and we fix what we can.

“Good bread takes time. Good neighbours take cake.”
